- say that your project is **based on** or a **fork of** DevCatz (or any Catzoid app), in plain text, and link to it;
- write about, review, teach or compare the apps, with screenshots of the real apps and their real names;
- build and run the unmodified apps for yourself, your team or your company, under their names;
- redistribute **unmodified** official builds under their names, with the licence and this file;
- use the names to say your software works **with** a Catzoid app ("a plugin for DBCatz", "imports DevCatz sites"),

- publish a **modified** version under a Catzoid name, or with the cat or the app icons. A fork must have **its own
  name and its own icon** that cannot be mistaken for a Catzoid app;
- use a Catzoid name or the cat for a product, a hosted service, a company, a domain, an app-store listing or an
  account name;
- sell or charge for anything under a Catzoid name or with the cat;
- suggest that Catzoid endorses or supports your project, or that it is official.

## When you fork

1. Rename the app (window titles, the About box, the bundle id `com.mrcatz.*`, the URL schemes such as `devcatz://`).
2. Replace the icons, the mood cats and the Ask mark with your own art.
3. Point updates, crash reports and downloads at your own servers, not at catzoid.com.
4. Keep the copyright notices and the licence, as the licence requires.

## Asking

For anything not covered here, ask first: open an issue in this repository titled "Trademark question". Permission is
given in writing, case by case, and can be withdrawn.
